Public Safety Guidelines

✅ DO'S

  • Drink frequent water even if not feeling thirsty.
  • Wear lightweight, light-colored, loose cotton clothing.
  • Use sunglasses, wide-brim hats, or umbrellas outdoors.
  • Use ORS, homemade drinks like lassi, torani, lemon water.
  • Keep pets in shaded areas with plenty of fresh water.

❌ DON'TS

  • Do not venture out barefoot or in direct sunlight during noon hours.
  • Do not leave children or pets inside parked vehicles.
  • Avoid high-protein food and heavy physical strenuous exertion.
  • Avoid alcohol, tea, coffee, and carbonated soft drinks (dehydrating).
  • Never ignore symptoms of dizziness or severe headache.
